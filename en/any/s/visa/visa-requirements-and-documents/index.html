From real experience

tipWhen applying for a visa for a summer program lasting one month with an invitation letter from a university, determine the correct visa category.
It is crucial to differentiate between a 'visit' visa and a tourist visa. Consult the official visa application center (e.g., VFS) website for precise definitions and requirements for each visa type to ensure you apply for the appropriate one.

how-toWhen applying for a visa and needing to document a name change due to marriage and subsequent divorce, you typically need to provide evidence of all name changes.
This includes: marriage certificates, divorce decrees, and potentially a birth certificate if it was used to establish the original name. For each change, you may need to show the document that caused the change (e.g., marriage certificate) and the document that confirmed the previous name (e.g., divorce decree). If original documents are lost, obtaining official copies or certificates from the relevant civil registry (like ЗАГС in Russia) is necessary. Some jurisdictions may require these documents to be translated by a certified translator. For Russian citizens, obtaining a marriage certificate proving the name change after divorce might require a personal visit to the civil registry office, even if initial applications can be made online via Gosuslugi.

ruleFor Schengen visa applications, individuals over 18 years old are generally required to provide an Extract from the Employment Record System (Выписка о трудовой деятельности из системы СФР).
This official document confirms employment history and is crucial for visa processing. It can be obtained through the Gosuslugi portal (requesting an extract) or at SFR/MFC centers. A physical work record book is not accepted as primary proof for most Schengen countries, though it might serve as supplementary evidence in rare cases. The primary document is not required if one's entire work history began and ended before the electronic system's widespread adoption (around 2019), and data wasn't transferred. In such exceptions, a physical work record book, additional certificates, or pension/service confirmations may be used. Always verify specific requirements with the relevant visa center or consulate as country-specific nuances exist.

ruleSocial welfare payments (pensions, benefits) are recognized as a legitimate source of income for visa financial proof.
To successfully use them, applicants must provide: 1. An official certificate detailing the type and amount of payments. 2. Bank statements clearly showing regular receipt of these payments. 3. Proof that the total amount of these payments is sufficient to cover both daily expenses in the home country and all costs associated with the planned trip. If social welfare payments are the applicant's only source of income and are deemed insufficient, the application may be weakened. It is advised to bolster such applications by: 1. Including a financial sponsor. 2. Demonstrating additional savings or assets.

If staying with friends/relatives in Prague for more than 3 days, documentation like 'Potvrzeni o zajisteni ubytovani' (confirmation of accommodation) and proof of property ownership or rental agreement from the host may be required. Formal registration with the police (Oddeleni pobytových agend) might be necessary within 3 working days. Failure to comply could result in a fine (up to 3000 CZK). ruleFor visa applications where a sponsor is covering the travel expenses, a sponsorship letter is required.
The sponsor must provide their financial documents (bank statements, proof of income) along with the letter. If the applicant and sponsor are traveling together, and the sponsor is providing all financial documentation, it's advisable to include a sponsorship letter for the sponsored individual. The exact requirements and formatting of sponsorship letters can vary, and it's recommended to check the specific guidelines of the embassy or consulate.

ruleWhen applying for a visa, it is often recommended to include copies of previous passports, especially if they contain relevant visa stamps or travel history.
For Australian visa applications, this typically involves providing scans of the main page and pages with visas or entry/exit stamps from significant countries. The purpose is to demonstrate a travel history. While major visas like those for the USA or UK are important, minor visas from countries like Turkey or Egypt might not be as crucial unless specifically requested. If you hold dual citizenship and have a passport from another country, it is mandatory to declare and submit this passport as well. Failure to disclose all passports can lead to visa refusal. The relevant section for these documents is usually labeled 'Evidence of the applicant's previous travel' or similar.

ruleTo apply for a Schengen visa with a sponsor: 1. **Sponsorship is permissible:** It's a common and acceptable practice if personal funds are insufficient. 2. **Documentation is key:** Proper documentation is crucial for visa approval. 3. **Varying requirements:** Rules for sponsorship differ by country and visa center; always verify specifics. 4. **Common Sponsors:** Typically parents, spouses, or close relatives.
Other sponsors might be accepted but require verification. 5. **Required Sponsor Documents:** * Sponsorship Letter (free form, in Russian, English, or the language of the country of application). * Copy of Sponsor's Passport. * Bank Statement showing sufficient funds. * Proof of Income (e.g., employment letter, 2-NDFL). 6. **Consulate Scrutiny:** Consulates assess the sponsor's financial capacity and stability of income. 7. **Financial Proof:** A sponsorship letter alone is insufficient; financial documents are mandatory to demonstrate adequate funds for the entire trip.

riskFor Russian citizens applying for Schengen visas, obtaining long-term (e.g., 5-year) multi-visas is extremely challenging.
Most applicants receive single-entry visas for specific travel dates. Factors that *may* increase the (still low) chances for multi-visas include having close relatives in the EU, a history of frequent Schengen visits, and strong guarantees of return. One reported success case for a 5-year Schengen visa involved a Russian citizen applying through VFS in the UAE, with a German citizen spouse. New EU regulations might offer better prospects for long-term multi-visas to Russian citizens permanently residing outside the Russian Federation, regardless of their Russian passport.

riskWhen traveling on a Schengen visa, it's crucial to demonstrate that the country which issued the visa is your primary destination (where you spend the most days).
For example, if holding a Greek visa but planning extensive travel in Germany, be prepared to prove longer stay in Greece. A common issue is the cancellation of hotel bookings in the visa-issuing country after entry; some Schengen member states are known to annul visas in such cases, even post-entry. Maintaining original hotel bookings for the duration stated in the visa application is highly recommended to avoid complications.

riskApplying for a tourist visa to one Schengen country while having an active residence permit application in another is legally complex.
While countries do not share real-time databases on pending residence permits, authorities may issue a stamp in the passport upon submission of a residence permit application. This stamp can alert border guards or visa officers, potentially leading to questions or refusals. Agencies often advise against simultaneous applications to avoid rejection risks.

caseIt is common for children's visa applications to be processed and approved faster than those of adults.
This is often due to simpler documentation requirements for minors, as their applications are less likely to be tied to employment status or complex financial histories. Consulates generally do not delay a child's visa issuance pending the approval of their parents' applications. If a child's documentation is complete and meets requirements, their visa is typically processed promptly. This does not necessarily indicate a negative outcome for the adult applicants; their processing may simply take longer.
