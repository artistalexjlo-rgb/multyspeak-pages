riskInsurance against visa refusal, often termed 'non-refundable ticket insurance' or 'travel disruption insurance,' typically covers situations where a visa is denied.
However, coverage details vary significantly by policy. One former insurance company employee noted that payouts might not be guaranteed if a visa is refused, depending on the specific terms and conditions outlined in the policy. It is crucial to read the policy document carefully to understand the exact coverage, required documentation for claims, and any exclusions or limitations.
