Application and Appointment Process in any: what actually matters to know

From real experience

tipHolding two valid passports can be a practical strategy for frequent travelers, especially when one passport is tied up during a visa application process.
While one passport is submitted to a consulate for a visa, the second, valid passport can be used for other travel. It is generally recommended to have two 10-year passports, as 5-year passports are less common for new issuances and may offer less utility. This dual-passport approach ensures continuous travel capability.

ruleSchengen visa group booking regulations vary by country: 1) Italy (Almaviva/VMS) and France allow group appointments (5-6 people) without proof of relation.
2) Spain offers family slots (up to 6 in Moscow, 2 in St. Petersburg) but strictly requires documented proof of relationship. 3) Portugal only allows individual slots. Premium/VIP lounge options range from 6,000 to 15,000 RUB depending on the visa center but do not accelerate processing times.

tipAdding an infant to a multi-airline ticket after the initial booking, especially when the infant was not yet born, is a common logistical challenge.
While some airlines (e.g., Qatar Airways) may offer free base fares for infants under two, taxes and fees still apply. This process usually requires direct contact with each operating airline or the booking agent's (e.g., Aviasales) technical support. It may involve reissuing tickets and coordinating details across different carriers, as aggregators may not always have a direct self-service option for this.

riskAirline ground staff, particularly in China, may lack up-to-date or comprehensive knowledge of international visa regulations, including Visa-on-Arrival (VoA) and visa-free agreements for various nationalities.
Travelers have reported being denied boarding even when eligible for entry without a pre-arranged visa. It is strongly recommended to carry official documentation (e.g., IATA Timatic printouts, embassy websites, official government travel advisories) detailing your visa requirements for your destination and nationality to present to airline personnel as proof.

tipExtended stopovers (e.g., 7-10 days) on international flights are possible but often increase the ticket price.
Simple stopovers are typically limited to around 1.5 days without significant cost increases. Some airlines might offer free hotel stays and visas for long layovers (over 12 hours), but this doesn't guarantee city access. Considering alternative routes through hubs like Cairo or Istanbul might be cost-effective, but be aware of potential security checks, especially in Istanbul.

how-toIf flight details change after submitting visa documents but before the visa is issued, you must immediately notify the consulate or visa center.
Do not wait until the trip. If the flight change forces the trip duration to exceed the issued visa's validity or violates the itinerary dates, you may need to rebook flights to match the visa dates or contact the consulate to request an amendment.
