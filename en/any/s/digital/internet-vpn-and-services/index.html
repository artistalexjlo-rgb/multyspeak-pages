riskCrypto exchanges (e.g., ByBit) enforce strict KYC and geo-restriction policies.
Accessing an account from a different country or using an eSIM/VPN with a flagged region can trigger automatic account locks. Users are often required to provide local tax IDs (like CPF in Brazil) or undergo re-verification. Recommended risk management: diversify assets across multiple platforms, keep a portion in cash/traditional bank accounts, and ensure network settings match the user's location status.
