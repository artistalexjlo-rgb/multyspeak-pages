riskBus transfers between Bulgaria (e.g., Sunny Beach, Burgas) and Istanbul Airport (IST), especially with companies like ArdaTur, are frequently unreliable.
Common issues include significant delays (2-3 hours due to border controls, traffic), and buses not entering the airport zone for passenger pickup despite tickets indicating airport arrival. Nishikli is generally considered more regular. It is highly risky to plan a flight with a tight connection (e.g., 3 hours) after a scheduled bus arrival. Passengers might need to travel to the main bus station or find alternative transport if the bus fails to pick up at the airport. It's advisable to allow substantial buffer time or consider an earlier bus/overnight stay in Istanbul.

Sending sweets/chocolates is also unpredictable. Generally, sending parcels from Russia to Germany carries a high risk. Parcels up to 8kg have been successfully delivered. A Russian Post office is reportedly available at the 'Russian House' in Berlin.

tipThe fastest way to travel from Venice to Vienna is by plane.
Train or bus travel is significantly longer, approximately 7 hours. If flying between Venice and Vienna (within the Schengen Area), there is typically no passport control. However, visa validity checks might occur. For transportation from Vienna International Airport (VIE) to the city center, options include express trains (like Railjet - RJ) which take about 15 minutes. There is no direct metro line from the airport to the city center, but other train services are available.

riskWhen planning train journeys, especially in Europe (e.g., with ÖBB), allow ample time for transfers.
A 4-minute transfer is highly risky due to potential train delays. Be aware that 'Sparschiene' type tickets are typically valid only for the specific train booked, unlike standard flexible tickets, meaning a missed connection requires a new ticket purchase. On large train stations, even with punctual arrivals, the time required to navigate between distant platforms can be significant.

riskIn Vienna and potentially other cities, be aware that public transport ticket inspections can be conducted by plainclothes officers as well as uniformed inspectors.

how-toFor bus travel from the Bulgarian Black Sea coast (e.g., Sunny Beach, Burgas) to Istanbul, note the following: - Arda Tour buses usually go directly to the Istanbul bus terminal and do not stop at Istanbul Airport (IST). - Nishikli buses generally do make a stop at Istanbul Airport (IST). - When purchasing tickets online, especially via platforms like Obilet.com, there might be discrepancies in showing the Istanbul Airport stop compared to the bus company's direct website.
Always verify the route with the bus company if the airport stop is critical. - From Istanbul Bus Terminal (Otogar) to Istanbul Airport (IST), the Havaist shuttle service is available and costs approximately 336 Turkish Lira.
