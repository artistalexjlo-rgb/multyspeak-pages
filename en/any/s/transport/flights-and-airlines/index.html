riskBe aware of potential flight schedule changes and cancellations when booking with some Chinese airlines, particularly Sichuan Airlines and Beijing Capital Airlines.
Significant changes, including route cancellations and day-long delays, can occur close to the departure date. While some airlines like Air China and China Southern Airlines may have better track records, it's advisable to monitor your bookings closely. It is recommended to buy tickets closer to the departure date for Sichuan Airlines if purchasing from them, as advance bookings have led to schedule changes.

Customer experiences suggest that final documents often correctly reflect a 20kg checked baggage and 5kg hand luggage allowance. However, it is always recommended to verify with the travel agent to confirm the exact baggage policy for the specific flight and booking, especially close to departure.

tipA '0/0' notation for baggage on an airline ticket may indicate a base fare without included baggage, or a system glitch.
This can occur even if baggage was purchased separately or is included by default for the specific route (e.g., flights from Russia to Cuba typically include baggage). Passengers should always refer to their baggage tags and airline confirmation for their actual baggage allowance, as the ticket display might be misleading or not updated.

how-toIf luggage is lost, immediately file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the airport.
Typically, found luggage is delivered to the hotel within a few days. If the traveler is returning to their home country, delivery to their residential address may be possible. The airline is responsible for compensation, not the airport. Travelers can request a voucher if a bank account is unavailable for compensation. Contact the airport's lost and found service and then the airline with the incident report to discuss delivery or compensation options.
