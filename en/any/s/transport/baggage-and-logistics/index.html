Baggage and Logistics in any: what actually matters to know

From real experience

caseThe debate between traveling with checked luggage versus carry-on only is discussed.
One perspective suggests traveling light, perhaps buying new clothes as needed, implying this is feasible for those with higher disposable income. The counter-argument emphasizes personal preference and the need for a varied wardrobe, making checked luggage more convenient for some, even with laundry services. The necessity of packing depends on individual needs, travel duration, and personal style, with the ability to purchase items en route being a factor. Some travelers even leave items behind or gift them to reduce luggage weight.

ruleMany airlines allow passengers to combine their baggage allowance up to 32 kg per item for two passengers.
Specifically, Emirates has been reported to allow this without issue. Turkish Airlines has also permitted exceeding individual limits in some cases. However, this policy might not apply to all destinations (e.g., Africa and America). Standard baggage limits for economy class are often 23 kg, while business class can be up to 30 kg. It is advisable to contact the airline's call center for current and specific baggage pooling and weight limit policies before travel.

caseCarry-on luggage regulations on ferries, such as the Donhae-Vladivostok route, can vary.
While official policies might specify weight limits (e.g., 25 kg for hand luggage combined with checked baggage), reviews suggest that carry-on items are often not weighed. Passengers can typically bring what they can manage to carry onto the ramp. It's advisable to check the specific ferry operator's policy for precise details, but generally, personal carry-on items that are manageable are permitted without strict weight checks.

European airlines are actively lobbying to revise these rules to reduce compensation payouts and remove the obligation to allow two free pieces of cabin baggage.

ruleAirline baggage allowances, especially for families with children, are typically determined by the purchased fare.
