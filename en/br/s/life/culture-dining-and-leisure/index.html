tipTo purchase football tickets in Brazil, it's recommended to register on the desired team's official website in advance, as sites may experience glitches.
Tickets can be bought online or at stadium ticket offices. Photo identification is required for entry, so all attendees must be registered. Prices vary significantly based on the sector, opponent, and purchase method. Examples: a corner sector ticket for Santos at Vila Belmiro cost 296R; a Flamengo match ticket at Maracana purchased via GetYourGuide cost 580R (includes organizer fees). Prices can be higher for rival team matches or visitor sections. An alternative is to buy a package through a local tour firm in Rio, which might include transfers and a guide for about 250R for a Maracana match.

tipTourists often confuse local wildlife at the Praça da República (Campo de Santana) in Rio de Janeiro.
Agoutis and coatis are frequently misidentified as capybaras by visitors at this location. While capybaras do exist in various urban parks in Brazil, they are not guaranteed to be found at this specific site.

tipTo cook delicious beans in Brazil, soak them overnight with half a lemon.
In the morning, cook them in a pressure cooker or simmer for a long time. Key to flavor is a rich mix of spices, including onions, carrots, and possibly 'shushu'. Salt should be added towards the end of cooking, as adding it early can make the beans tough. For a richer dish, meat or sausages can be added. 'Ervilha' refers to peas, not beans.
