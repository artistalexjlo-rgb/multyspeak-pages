Regional Travel Routes and Tips: how it works in Brazil — first-hand

Short answer
For an ideal Brazilian itinerary, combine the cultural hubs of São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro with natural highlights like Iguazu Falls, Lençóis Maranhenses, and the historical town of Paraty. Prioritize Rio for its superior beaches and lifestyle, while using São Paulo as your primary functional business and transportation hub.

During Carnival, there's a common perception that São Paulo offers a quieter and safer alternative to Rio de Janeiro.
However, São Paulo also hosts its own extensive street carnivals ('blocos'), which can be chaotic and crowded. Rio's Carnival features both large parades and impressive papier-mâché art installations. Travelers should expect vibrant, potentially overwhelming, festivities in both major cities during Carnival.

For a two-week itinerary in Brazil starting from São Paulo, consider flying to key destinations like Rio de Janeiro, Salvador, Foz do Iguaçu, and Florianopolis.
Additionally, a highly recommended historical town is Paraty, located on the coast. From Paraty, one can take a schooner (escuna) trip to explore wild beaches; 'Netuno I' or 'Netuno II' are recommended schooners for such excursions.

In Brazil, proximity to cities like São Sebastião is appealing, but car ownership is essential.
Travel time to São Paulo is approximately 3 hours, offering a convenient connection. The lifestyle in some areas can be perceived as more suited for retirees. Alternative locations like Recife and João Pessoa are considered, but with significant reservations.

Rio de Janeiro is highly rated as a top city in Latin America for family life, offering beautiful beaches, pleasant weather, diverse architecture, and cultural vibrancy.
While Sao Paulo (SP) is preferred for business networking and serves as a major transportation hub (USA, Europe, Africa, Turkey), Rio is favored for its lifestyle. Florianopolis is also mentioned as a good option.

When choosing a city for relocation to Brazil, consider Itajaí for its calm atmosphere, moderate climate, and opportunities for physical work.
It is a good option for those who prefer smaller cities and value a good quality of life with access to activities like fishing. If Itajaí seems too large, a more remote district of Florianópolis could offer a quieter alternative.

Fortaleza, with a population of about 2.5 million, is highly praised for its ocean, beaches, and waterfront, making it an excellent destination for beach vacations.
However, cleanliness varies, and garbage piles can be found. The Russian-speaking community in Fortaleza is small, estimated at around 20 people. It is considered a good option for those seeking a beach-centric lifestyle and a relaxed atmosphere.

Londrina, Brazil, is described as a calm, clean, and safe city, making it a suitable destination for travelers prioritizing security and a relaxed atmosphere over extensive tourist attractions.
Its location also facilitates travel to other major cities and points of interest.

When choosing a city for an internship in Brazil, consider Curitiba for better infrastructure and a more predictable urban environment.
Cities in the Northeast (Maceió, Aracaju) offer better beaches but significantly less cultural and transit connectivity compared to the South and Southeast regions.

São Paulo is recognized as a major business hub and a center for events and transportation.
However, some visitors find its overall atmosphere ("vibe") less appealing, especially when compared to Rio de Janeiro. For these individuals, São Paulo's main draw is its functional role as a business and transport hub.

Logistics to Lençóis Maranhenses: Fly from Belém to São Luís (~1 hour).
Take a 4.5-hour shuttle (minivan) to Barreirinhas. From Barreirinhas, join a guided 4x4 tour to the dunes. An alternative starting point for excursions is Santo Amaro (1.5 hours from Barreirinhas).

For travel to Brazil in September, Lençóis Maranhenses is recommended.
For New Year's (December-January), Chapada Diamantina is suggested as an alternative, due to concerns that the lagoons in Lençóis Maranhenses might be dry during this period.

Santos beaches in Brazil offer better facilities (toilets, foot washes, showers) and high police presence in summer.
For a winter day trip, Paranapiacaba, a museum-city 60-80 km from São Paulo, is recommended.
