How to Obtain a CPF in Brazil: what actually matters to know

From real experience

The CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas) is a local Brazilian tax identification number essential for several services.
While daily purchases can usually be made without it, a CPF is often required for: purchasing a local SIM card (easier in large shopping malls or specialized offices for foreigners); using certain ride-hailing apps (e.g., 99, while Uber sometimes works with international credit cards); and acquiring public transport cards (with exceptions like Florianopolis' tourist card). It can be obtained quickly in Brazil (15-30 minutes processing, 1-3 days issuance) by presenting a passport. Obtaining CPF via embassies prior to arrival can be problematic due to potential data errors or refusal of service.

For short-term visitors (e.g., one week) in Brazil, obtaining a CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas) is often not strictly necessary unless deep engagement with local systems is planned.
Online applications from outside Brazil are usually unsuccessful, redirecting to consulates or physical offices. Internal flight tickets can sometimes be purchased without CPF on official airline websites (e.g., Azul) by selecting 'estrangeiro' (foreigner) and using a passport number. Other platforms may require CPF or make it difficult without one, leading to higher prices on third-party sites. The most reliable way to obtain a CPF is in person at a Receita Federal (tax office) in Brazil, which can be completed within 5-7 business days or even the same day.

It is no longer possible to remotely apply for a CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas) in Brazil for foreigners.
While the option may still appear on the website, direct online application is unavailable for non-residents. The process now typically requires an in-person visit to a tax office in Brazil or to the Brazilian consulate in your home country. Required documents include a passport, application form, flight tickets, and a birth certificate. It is advisable to confirm the exact procedure and required documents with the relevant Brazilian authorities.

Previously, it was possible to apply at the Brazilian Consulate in Moscow by filling out a form and awaiting an invitation for an appointment. However, recent information suggests that the Moscow Consulate now requires a work or study visa for CPF applications. Alternative methods: 1. Apply in Brazil: This is now considered the primary method. Be aware that if you are a tourist with a short-term stay, you might be denied a CPF if deemed unnecessary. However, with proper documentation, it should be obtainable. 2. Apply at another country's Brazilian Consulate: This is a less common and potentially difficult option, as third-country consulates may require local residency (e.g., a visa or local ID) and may not serve tourists from other nations. One user reported obtaining a CPF at the Brazilian Consulate in Sri Lanka, but they had a resident visa.

CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas) application requirements for Russian citizens in Brazil can be complex and vary.
While some sources indicate that an apostilled birth certificate is not required for CPF itself, it may be necessary for further processes like obtaining a residence permit (VNH). For online applications or applications at Brazilian embassies abroad, documents like a birth certificate and translation may be requested. However, obtaining CPF as a tourist at the Receita Federal office with a passport might be a simpler process. Always verify the latest requirements before applying.

Process for obtaining a CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas) in Brazil.
General steps involve online application, approval, scheduling an in-person visit, and subsequent issuance. Variations exist, with some users reporting issues with document submission via email and others successfully using an online portal to upload documents after creating an account and receiving an FCPF code. In-person visits to Receita Federal are also possible but may require prior online scheduling. CPF may be issued in paper or digital format, with a trend towards digital issuance since 2021. Some users reported receiving paper copies in Russia.

When moving to Brazil, avoid submitting your CPF (tax ID) application during short transit stays in a different state (e.g., in São Paulo for 1-2 days before flying to João Pessoa).
Since CPF registration issues the final number via email a few days later, registering it under a temporary out-of-state address can cause synchronization errors in federal databases once you relocate, rendering the CPF temporarily unreadable. Apply directly in your final destination city instead.

Online or mail-in applications are frequently rejected or processed with strict conditions. Applicants must appear in person within 10 days of submitting an initial request, or the application will be automatically canceled.

Foreigners and tourists can register a CPF physically at local offices (Receita Federal) and are free to use a different address when applying to open local bank accounts along their travel route.
