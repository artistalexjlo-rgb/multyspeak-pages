Transport and Logistics: how it works in Sri Lanka — first-hand

From real experience

riskTo legally drive a scooter/motorcycle in Sri Lanka, an International Driving Permit (IDP) with category A is generally required.
Additionally, as of August 2025, a local temporary permit is also needed, which can be obtained upon arrival at the airport. Driving without a valid category A license (or IDP/local permit) can result in fines ranging from 1,000 to 25,000 LKR (approx. 3-80 USD). The owner of the rental bike may also face fines. Renters often take a deposit to cover potential penalties. While official fines are high, many situations are resolved on the spot with a payment of 2,000-5,000 LKR. Police stops for tourists are common.

riskIn Sri Lanka, there is no commonly known '19 km/h grace' for speeding fines.
While tourists might not always be strictly monitored for minor speeding due to road conditions limiting speed, traffic laws are in place. A significant rule change/enforcement update was implemented around April-May, making seatbelt usage strictly enforced. Fines can be issued for both the driver and the front-seat passenger if not wearing a seatbelt. Tour guides are generally aware of these stricter enforcement measures. Speed limits are indicated, especially on toll roads.

tipTransportation between Jaffna and Trincomalee in Sri Lanka can be challenging.
Direct train services are unavailable, with travel typically routed through Colombo. Local buses are an option but may lack amenities like air conditioning and reclining seats, with journeys potentially lasting around 6 hours. Alternative routes via Dambulla exist but involve transfers and extended travel times. For more convenience, private taxis or specialized transfer services are recommended. Checking with local bus stations or travel agencies for the most up-to-date schedules and routes is advisable.

riskTaxi drivers using ride-hailing apps like Pick Me Up or Uber in Sri Lanka may ask passengers to cancel the ride in the app to avoid paying the service commission (which can range from 7% to 20%).
This practice removes the trip from the passenger's app history, making it difficult to resolve potential disputes. Passengers are strongly advised not to comply with such requests. If cancellation is unavoidable, take a screenshot of the trip details (cost, route) beforehand. For cash payments, the driver is responsible for remitting the commission to the service.

caseWhen flying back from Sri Lanka (e.g., via Aeroflot), passengers can transport fruits in both checked baggage and carry-on luggage.
Sturdy fruits like pineapples and mangoes can be wrapped in paper and packed in checked bags. Delicate fruits (rambutans, mangosteens) are best transported in carry-on boxes (up to 5 kg). Airline staff at Colombo Airport (CMB) may have varying levels of strictness: some shifts strictly enforce the 23kg checked bag limit, while others tolerate slight overweights up to 25.4kg without charging extra.

tipStandard mail between Sri Lanka and Russia takes approximately two weeks.
For urgent or sensitive documents, it is highly recommended to avoid standard postal services to reduce the risk of loss. The most reliable and fastest method is to entrust documents to travelers flying directly between the two countries or to use specialized international courier services (DHL/FedEx/UPS), though availability may vary by region.

tipTo travel from Unawatuna or Galle to Ella, it is not necessary to go via Colombo unless planning intermediate stops like Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, or Pinnawala.
Direct transportation options include buses (economical, with stops), private car hire (most comfortable), or scooter/motorbike rental (for independent travel). There are no direct non-stop buses from Galle to Ella; buses will make stops.

tipProvide advice on motorbike travel in Sri Lanka.
Confirm that daytime exploration by motorbike is feasible and generally safe, particularly on the south coast. Advise caution for nighttime travel due to poor lighting and reduced activity. Recommend using ride-sharing apps like PickMe for evening transport and suggest seeking information from specialized online communities for bike travel.

caseFuel is readily available for tourists in Weligama, Sri Lanka.
While prices may have increased slightly, tourists can purchase gasoline without queues or QR codes. Unlike locals who might face limitations, tourists can typically buy up to 10 liters of fuel without issues. This indicates a supportive approach by local authorities towards the tourism sector, ensuring convenient access to fuel for visitors.

riskIn highly popular tourist locations on Sri Lanka (such as Coconut Tree Hill in Mirissa), local tuk-tuk drivers ('tuk-tuk mafia') may physically intercept or intimidate app-based PickMe drivers, forcing them to cancel the ride or park far away.
To avoid this, walk 300–500 meters away from the local tuk-tuk stands before ordering a ride through PickMe or Uber.

how-toTo check train schedules in Sri Lanka, use `railway.gov.lk` or `Rome2Rio`.
Be aware that published schedules may not always be accurate on the day of travel, and cancellations (e.g., morning trains) can occur without prior online notice. It is advisable to arrive at the station early and be prepared for potential changes or alternative transport arrangements.

tipSoursop (graviola) is highly perishable and difficult to transport.
Unripe fruits often fail to ripen properly post-flight, while ripe ones rot quickly. The best practice is to ask the local seller to select fruits specifically for export/flight; they can identify the exact maturity level needed to survive the journey and ripen safely at home.

Six months prior, it was 150 USD. One user also mentioned 150 EUR. Payment is typically made at the check-in counter, often in USD or EUR.

how-toTrain from Colombo to Kandy route: The train goes directly to Rambukkana.
From Rambukkana to Kandy, it's a 3-4 km ride by tuk-tuk. Some hotels offer free tuk-tuk transfers from Rambukkana station. Check the Sri Lankan Railways website (Rdmns.lk) for schedules.

tipBus travel from Trincomalee to Passikudah often involves private minibuses heading towards Ampara.
Online booking is usually unavailable. Tickets must be purchased in person at the bus station. Passengers should monitor the route via map applications to alight at the correct stop before the final destination.
