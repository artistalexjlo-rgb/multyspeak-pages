South and Southwest Coast Beaches in Sri Lanka: what actually matters to know

Short answer
The East Coast, particularly Trincomalee and Arugam Bay, offers calm, clear waters and ideal weather from May to September. In contrast, the South and West coasts experience monsoon conditions with rough seas during these months, though they feature better infrastructure and more tourist amenities year-round.

From real experience

When comparing Sri Lanka's East and South Coasts for tourism: The South (e.g., Hikkaduwa, Mirissa, Unawatuna, Bentota) offers developed infrastructure, diverse dining (including Western options), and numerous tourist amenities.
However, in July, it may feature strong waves and less clear ocean water. The East (e.g., Trincomalee, Uppuveli, Arugam Bay) is known for calm, clear oceans in July and abundant wildlife. It has less developed infrastructure, fewer diverse cuisine options, and potentially more challenging conditions for scooter rentals and organized tours. Arugam Bay is a prominent surfing destination located approximately 8 hours from Colombo. For longer stays, visiting both regions is recommended to experience their distinct characteristics.

From May to July, the weather seasons in Sri Lanka shift significantly.
The West and South-West coasts (e.g., Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Unawatuna) experience the monsoon season, featuring rough, muddy oceans, high waves, and frequent rains (mostly at night/morning). Conversely, the East Coast (Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay) enters its prime dry season. Trincomalee is highly recommended for families due to calm beaches with no waves and sufficient restaurants. Arugam Bay is the main hub for surfing and nightlife during this period.

Unawatuna beach in Sri Lanka, while popular, has limitations.
It is a small beach with grey sand, and can become crowded, especially on weekends. The water is shallow and calm, but not always clear. Swimming is restricted to a designated area, and venturing beyond is not advised. For a quieter experience, alternative beaches might be preferable, especially during peak season.

When traveling to Sri Lanka in January/winter with children, ocean waves on the south coast can be very strong.
Avoid open beaches and search for hotels located in protected bays or behind natural reefs. The best spots for wave-free swimming during this season are Unawatuna, certain reef-protected parts of Hikkaduwa, and the sheltered corners of Mirissa.
