Paying via QR Codes (Russian Banks) in Vietnam: real experience from chats

Short answer
Russian travelers can pay via QR codes (VietQR/VNPAY) using apps like Sberbank, VTB, and MTS, though acceptance is selective and transactions may take up to 90 seconds. For cash, VRB ATMs support MIR cards, while UnionPay cards offer broader acceptance at ATMs and retailers. Always carry backup cash, as digital payments remain intermittently unstable and require a stable internet connection.
