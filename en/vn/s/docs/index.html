Documentation Support: how it works in Vietnam — first-hand

From real experience

ruleWhen a minor (e.g., 15 years old) travels internationally with one parent and has a different last name, a notarized consent letter from the absent parent is generally not required for transit through countries like China or entry into Vietnam, especially if they have their own passport.
A translated birth certificate is also typically not necessary. However, regulations can vary, so it's always best to confirm with the relevant consulates or immigration authorities of the countries involved.

ruleChanging your surname after marriage does not immediately invalidate your passport for travel, especially for visa-free destinations like Vietnam.
While Russian law allows six months to update documents, many individuals travel with their existing passport until its expiry without issues at immigration. Showing the valid passport is the primary requirement. Carrying the marriage certificate is recommended as a precautionary measure, though it may not be explicitly required for entry into Vietnam.

ruleAccording to Decree 288/2025/NĐ-CP (effective Nov 5, 2025), Vietnam mandates strict rules for UAVs/drones.
All drones require registration. Operating devices over 250g requires an operator's license and explicit flight approval for every single launch. Foreigners must secure a Vietnamese citizen or local organization as a guarantor to process permits. Flying without authorization risks confiscation and fines up to $2000.

ruleRegarding the digital arrival card (e-Arrival) for Vietnam: Initially, it was mandatory only for Ho Chi Minh City.
There are reports that starting June 1st, it might be required for all provinces. However, for Cam Ranh specifically, there is uncertainty, with some information suggesting it's not currently required but could be implemented from July. The official website can be found via Google search. Non-compliance consequences are unclear for provinces other than Ho Chi Minh City.

ruleApplying for a Schengen visa from Vietnam is possible through official visa centers (like VFS Global) or local visa agencies if you possess a Vietnamese residence permit.
If you lack a residence permit, you must apply in your country of permanent residence or citizenship. International agents and Russian tour operators may also offer assistance, though often geared towards applications from Russia.

ruleIn Vietnam, when there's a discrepancy between the visa/entry stamp duration and an email notification from immigration regarding the permitted stay, trust the stamp.
The entry stamp placed in your passport at immigration is the official record of your allowed duration of stay. Arrival cards may not always be explicitly requested as the information might be electronically linked.

riskThe ability to obtain a new Russian international passport while having outstanding loan debts depends on specific legal circumstances and potential travel bans issued by Russian authorities.
Generally, significant debts could lead to restrictions on passport issuance. It is advisable to consult with official Russian passport services or a legal advisor for clarification on individual cases.

tipWhen traveling internationally with a child who has a different surname, ensure you have supporting documentation.
While Vietnamese immigration may not rigorously check, Russian border control might request a birth certificate to confirm kinship. It is advisable to carry the child's birth certificate, especially if surnames differ, to avoid potential complications at the border.

ruleExplain that hostels in Vietnam legally require guests to present a valid passport and proof of legal stay (e-visa or entry stamp) for check-in and registration with local authorities.
Confirm that this is a standard procedure across accommodations.

ruleIn Vietnam, a Russian Category B driver's license is insufficient for renting motorcycles or scooters.
Only electric scooters or those under 50cc can be ridden without a specific license. To rent and ride motorcycles or scooters above 50cc, a Category A license from Russia is required.

Refund was denied. Inquire about the policy on minor spelling errors on train tickets in Vietnam and the likelihood of being allowed to travel with a one-letter mistake.

how-toIf you have booked flight tickets with an old or incorrect passport number and have since obtained a new passport, it is often possible to update the passenger details with the airline.
This process usually involves a fee and may depend on the airline's policy and the time remaining until your flight. Contact the airline's customer support for assistance. Be aware that ticket prices can increase significantly if you wait until closer to the departure date.
