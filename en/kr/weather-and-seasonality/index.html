Weather and Climate in South Korea: what actually matters to know

Short answer
South Korea experiences intense heat, high humidity, and frequent rain throughout July and August, making indoor attractions a more comfortable alternative to outdoor activities. Travelers should be prepared for cooler-than-expected sea temperatures and are advised to avoid heat-sensitive destinations like Daegu during the peak summer period.

From real experience

The weather in South Korea during July and August is characterized by intense heat and humidity, often exacerbated by rain.
Outdoor activities can be very uncomfortable, feeling like a 'bath.' Many locals and residents seek refuge in air-conditioned indoor spaces. The natural scenery is less appealing than in spring or autumn. Traveling to coastal areas like Donghae or Sokcho might be an option, but the sea can be rough, limiting swimming. Indoor attractions are a more comfortable alternative during this period.

Sea temperatures in South Korea during August can be cooler than expected, even with daytime air temperatures around 23°C, due to cold ocean currents.
Unlike tropical destinations like Thailand, the water rarely reaches consistently warm temperatures suitable for swimming. Additionally, August falls within the rainy season, with frequent rainfall possible, particularly in early July. Travelers seeking a purely beach-focused vacation might consider alternative destinations or be prepared for cooler water conditions.

Late July and August in Seoul are characterized by high temperatures and humidity, often referred to as the peak summer heat.
While the intensity of the rainy season may decrease towards the end of July, the heat and humidity persist. Visitors sensitive to extreme heat may find this period uncomfortable. The heat typically begins to subside in late September.

South Korea's August is characterized by intense heat, high humidity, and the rainy season.
UV-protective umbrellas are highly recommended. Early September is generally more pleasant for outdoor activities, even though beach infrastructure (loungers/umbrellas) is typically removed after late August.

The weather in Jeju during mid to late November is described as suitable for an autumn trip, offering cool temperatures and scenic foliage.
While early November can still be warm, temperatures decrease towards the end of the month. Jeju can maintain summer-like temperatures until early December.

Daegu experiences extremely high temperatures during the summer due to its location in a basin.
Temperatures can be very intense, making it uncomfortable for tourists during the peak summer season. It is advisable to avoid visiting Daegu in the summer if sensitive to heat.
