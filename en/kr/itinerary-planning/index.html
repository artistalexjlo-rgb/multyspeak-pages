Itinerary Planning in South Korea: what actually matters to know

Short answer
Travelers must possess a valid K-ETA or visa, confirmed return tickets, accommodation bookings, and a detailed daily itinerary. It is essential to carry approximately $100 USD in physical cash per day and ensure all application details match your statements to avoid potential entry denial.

From real experience

To ensure smooth entry into South Korea, travelers must possess specific documents and be prepared for potential immigration checks.
Essential requirements include: 1. **Sufficient Funds:** Carry cash equivalent to at least $100 USD per day of stay. While card statements might be requested, physical cash is prioritized. 2. **Return Tickets:** Possess confirmed return tickets to your country of citizenship or residence. If traveling to a third country, be prepared to provide a convincing reason. 3. **Accommodation Proof:** Have a confirmed booking for a hotel or rental (e.g., Airbnb). 4. **Travel Itinerary:** Prepare a detailed day-by-day itinerary, especially for short stays, outlining at least three logical activities per day. This demonstrates a genuine tourist intent. 5. **K-ETA or Visa:** Ensure you have a valid K-ETA (for eligible nationalities) or the appropriate visa. The information provided in your K-ETA application (purpose of visit, employment details) must accurately reflect your situation upon arrival. Failure to meet any of these requirements can lead to denial of entry. It is advisable to have these documents readily accessible but only present them if requested by immigration officers to avoid appearing suspicious.
