Updating K-ETA Trip Details in South Korea: what actually matters to know

Short answer
You do not need to reapply for a K-ETA for subsequent trips; instead, simply update your accommodation, flight details, and travel dates on the official website before each entry. Ensure your K-ETA information remains consistent with your travel documents and itinerary to prevent issues at immigration.

From real experience

K-ETA information (such as accommodation address and companions) can be updated via the official K-ETA website even after approval.
For travelers arriving on separate flights, it is not strictly necessary to list companions in the K-ETA, but be prepared to provide the companion's name and flight details to immigration officers at the border if questioned.

The K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) is generally valid for 3 years.
If your travel plans change after obtaining K-ETA, you must update the information, including travel dates, accommodation details (hotel name), and companions. If you are traveling on a D-2 visa, any existing K-ETA should be canceled. It's crucial that the information provided in your K-ETA application accurately reflects your travel purpose (e.g., tourism vs. visiting friends). Upon arrival, be prepared to show proof of funds (approximately $100 USD per day in cash), return tickets, and a confirmed accommodation booking. Having a planned itinerary can also be beneficial.

For multiple entries into South Korea within the validity period of your K-ETA, it is not necessary to reapply.
Instead, update the travel dates and accommodation details within your existing K-ETA application before each new entry. This applies even for transit stays, such as a few days in Seoul en route to another destination. Children under 17 years of age do not require a K-ETA but may need to complete an arrival card.
