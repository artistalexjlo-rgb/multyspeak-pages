IST Airport to Bulgarian Resorts in Bulgaria: what actually matters to know

Short answer
Purchase tickets in advance via Obilet or at the station, as buses to Bulgaria fill up quickly. Direct services like Nishikli depart from Istanbul Airport floor -2, but taking a Havaist shuttle to the main Esenler Otogar is more reliable. Be prepared for 7-9 hour total travel times, including significant potential delays at the border.

From real experience

When traveling by bus from Istanbul to Bulgaria, it is highly recommended to purchase tickets in advance, especially during peak times, as buses are often full. * **Arda Bus (Istanbul-Burgas)**: Departs from Esenler Bus Terminal.
Adult ticket: 27 EUR, child ticket: 15 EUR. Travel time: approx. 7-8 hours, including border crossing (which can take 1.5 hours or more). Arda does not pick up from Istanbul Airport. * **Nishikli Bus (Istanbul Airport-Bulgaria)**: Direct services from Istanbul Airport (level -2, platform 19, near Nishikli kiosk) are available at 06:00 and 15:30. Evening services have historically been unavailable. * **Metro Bus**: Does not offer direct services from Istanbul Airport. * **Havaist**: Services run from Istanbul Airport (level -2, platform 5) approximately hourly. IstanbulCard purchase for Havaist can be declined; payment via bank card is an option. * **Ticket Purchase & Availability**: Check and purchase tickets on obilet.com. * **Transfer to Esenler Bus Terminal from Istanbul Airport**: Havaist5 operates from Istanbul New Airport (IST) to Esenler Otogarı.

To travel from Istanbul Airport (IST) to Bulgaria, several options exist.
Buses operated by companies like Nişli are available. Tickets can be purchased via obilet.com or, in some cases, booked via WhatsApp (though booking availability may be limited). The cost is approximately 30 EUR, payable by foreign bank cards. While Obilet may display prices in TRY, payment often requires a non-Russian card. The Havaist-5 bus route goes to Istanbul's main bus terminal (Otogar); tickets cost 330-336 TRY and can be paid with a foreign card or Istanbulkart. Direct purchase from the driver may not be possible. For those needing to pay in Turkish Lira, currency exchange is available at the airport.

To travel from Istanbul New Airport (IST) to Sveti Vlas, Bulgaria, several options exist: 1. **Direct Bus Services**: Nishikli Turizm offers buses departing at 06:00 and 15:30 (reportedly from Terminal 19).
In previous years, these buses made a stop in Sveti Vlas, but it is advisable to confirm current stops. Arda Tur offers buses to Varna (approximately 36 EUR or 70.41 BGN), with departures around 20:00. These might require a connection to Sveti Vlas or checking if they make an intermediate stop. 2. **Private/Group Transfers**: Group transfers cost approximately 70-80 EUR per person, while individual transfers can cost around 220 EUR.

For travel from Istanbul Airport to Bulgaria via bus, be aware of the specific routes and schedules.
Buses depart from the main bus station and stop at the airport. There are waiting areas available, such as on the lower level near platform 19, with departures typically at 6 AM and 3:30 PM. It's advisable to wait at the stop in advance as the bus makes brief stops. Luggage is handled with tags for identification, and tickets are checked. Note that private sellers might refuse older Euro banknotes.

Bus routes from Istanbul Airport to Bulgaria (e.g., Arda Tur) often show up on aggregators like Obilet with an 'airport' pickup, but in reality, many buses do not enter the airport premises.
It is more reliable to take a Havaist shuttle to the main bus station (otogar) and depart from there. Direct buses from the airport are very infrequent (e.g., 05:45, 15:30 for some carriers) and should not be relied upon if they don't match the travel schedule.

Bus travel between Bulgaria (Sunny Beach) and Istanbul Airport (IST) takes approximately 8-9 hours total.
Border processing time fluctuates between 1 and 3 hours due to customs system volatility (fingerprinting/photos). Nishiqli bus tickets cost 1500 TRY and must be purchased via the official ticket counter at the airport, not from the driver. Buses typically depart from the Shell gas station in Sunny Beach.
