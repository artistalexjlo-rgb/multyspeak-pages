Documentation Support in Bulgaria: what actually matters to know

From real experience

ruleBulgarian citizens can travel within the EU using a valid passport or national ID card (lichna karta), even if their validity period is nearing expiration.
The crucial requirement is that the document must not be expired at the time of travel. For long-term planning, 10-year validity passports are available.
✓ 2 reports from chats
ruleFor Russian citizens residing in Bulgaria, renewal of a Russian international passport at the Russian Consulate in Sofia officially takes up to 3 months, but typically completes within 2 to 2.5 months.
The status can be tracked on the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs website. For Bulgarian citizens, passport renewal times in Bulgaria are significantly faster. According to information on the MVR.bg website (under 'Дейности' -> 'Административно обслужване' -> 'Издаване на паспорт на български граждани'), options include 'Експрес поръчка' (express order) in 8 hours, 'бърза' (fast) in up to 3 days, and 'обикновена' (standard) in up to 1 month. It is crucial to distinguish between procedures for Russian and Bulgarian passports and consult the relevant authorities.

ruleWhen transferring property ownership in Bulgaria to children (as direct heirs), expect total costs to be approximately 4-7% of the property's price.
This typically includes: a local donation tax ranging from 0.1% to 5% (depending on the municipality, with minimal rates for direct heirs); notary fees of 0.5% to 1% of the transaction sum plus VAT; a state duty ('вписване') of 0.1%; fees for notary, translator, and document preparation services estimated at 350-500 EUR; and registration fees (Bulstat and tax authority) of approximately 100-150 EUR.

riskIn Bulgaria, all real estate transactions exceeding 10,000 BGN (approximately 5,000 EUR) must legally be conducted via bank transfer, as mandated since 2015. Cash payments for such amounts are illegal.
Engaging in schemes like understating the property price in the contract and paying the difference in cash is unlawful and can lead to the transaction being declared void. Russian tax authorities (FNS) can impose penalties up to 40% for undeclared foreign cash transactions related to property sales.

how-toExplains how to fill out the 'employer' section in a visa application when working under a Civil Law Contract (GPD).
It advises to list 'Contractor', 'Freelancer', or 'Consultant' as the position and the name of the contracting company as the employer. It also mentions attaching supporting documents like a 2-NDFL certificate (if applicable) and emphasizes that consulates focus on income stability and clarity of the source rather than the contract type itself.

how-toRequired documents for an individual entrepreneur (IP) applying for a visa: 1. Extract from EGRIP.
2. Certificate of tax registration. 3. Certificate of pension fund registration. 4. Electronic work book extract (10-year history). 5. Bank turnover report for the last 6 months. 6. Proof of pension fund contributions. Most documents can be printed via the Gosuslugi portal.

tipFor individuals needing to obtain medical documents quickly in Sofia for marriage registration between a Russian and Bulgarian citizen, a paid clinic is recommended.
The clinic 'Torax' is mentioned as a potential option. It is advised to check their website for specific services and timelines. It is unclear if the Bulgarian citizen needs to visit their personal physician or can use a private clinic for these documents.

how-toRequirements for bringing a pet into Bulgaria from non-EU countries: 1. International veterinary passport.
2. Microchip. 3. Valid rabies vaccination. 4. Rabies antibody titration test (must be done at least 3 months prior to travel). 5. Form F1 certificate from local veterinary authorities, exchanged for an EU health certificate at the border/airport.

how-toWhen inquiring about Bulgarian visa status in Moscow, email infomos@bul.com, Embassy.Moscow.bg, and secretary.ru simultaneously.
Include document submission date, full name as in the passport, and passport number. Responses are typically expected within 2-3 business days. For family reunification visas (Type D), only the official residence permission is required; financial/housing proofs are unnecessary.

how-toWhen filling out a visa application form for Bulgaria, questions regarding occupation such as 'homemaker' or 'unemployed' and 'individual entrepreneur' or specific profession should be clarified with the visa center.
It is advisable to prepare alternative answers or consult with the visa center staff for accurate guidance.

ruleUkrainian citizens aged 16 and older can travel to Bulgaria independently without parental consent.
For Russian citizens, it is recommended to carry a notarized parental consent form, as it serves as a standard precautionary travel document for minors, although enforcement at the Bulgarian border is generally lenient.

ruleIf a child is traveling with one parent and the other parent is not accompanying them, and the child is not a property owner, a notarized consent letter from the non-traveling parent is typically required.
For the traveling spouse of a property owner, no specific consent is usually needed.

riskWhen applying for a visa as an unemployed individual, an extract from the work record book (трудовая книжка), including electronic versions, is a mandatory document.
Attempting to apply as unemployed while officially employed may lead to issues if this requirement is not met.
